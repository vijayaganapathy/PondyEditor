Questions we often hear

Not here? Call or WhatsApp us and we will answer the same day.

What do you need from me to start?

A short brief: what you sell, who your customers are and what you want people to do after watching. We plan the rest with you.

Do you shoot the videos or only edit?

Both. We can shoot at your location and edit, or edit footage you already have.

Which formats will I receive?

Vertical for Reels, Shorts and WhatsApp Status, plus square and wide versions for Facebook and YouTube.

Can I ask for changes after the first draft?

Yes. You review the draft and tell us what to change. Revisions are part of our process.

How does Meta ads pricing work?

There are two parts: the ad budget you pay to Meta, and our service fee. We agree both with you before any campaign goes live.

Where do you work?

We are based in Thavalakuppam, Puducherry, and shoot locally. We also edit videos and run ads online for clients anywhere.
